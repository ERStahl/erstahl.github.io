ENTRY 006 — THE LEDGER GAME

In development

Keep the sum even. Or someone gets struck.

A tabletop role-playing game set in the world of the Register, five centuries before The Drift. You play a Scribe, an Exchequer, or one of the Struck — and the only resource that counts is years of life. Every year taken from one line must be added to another. The register must balance. The Censor is not watching for mistakes. He is watching for a count that comes out too perfect.

Same world. Same ledger. No heroes — only arithmetic.
